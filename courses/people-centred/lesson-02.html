People-Centred Smart Cities

Gehl’s three qualities of good public space

11 minutes · Free

Video lesson (placeholder)

Jan Gehl and his team developed a simple but powerful framework for evaluating public space. Every good public space should offer three things:

🛡️

Protection

From traffic, crime, weather, and noise

😊

Comfort

Places to walk, stand, sit, see, talk, play

✨

Enjoyment

Scale, climate, positive sensory experience

When you design or evaluate a street, square or park, ask yourself: Does this place protect people? Does it make them comfortable? Does it invite them to stay and enjoy?

In the next lesson we will look at how to measure these qualities systematically using the Public Space Public Life method.
