People-Centred Smart Cities

Applying the principles in Ulaanbaatar & beyond

8 minutes · Free

Video lesson (placeholder)

The same principles that work in Copenhagen or Melbourne can be applied in Ulaanbaatar — but only if they are adapted to local climate, culture and urban form.

Cold winters change the meaning of “protection”. Traditional ger districts change the meaning of “public space”. People-centred design in Mongolia must start from these realities rather than importing finished solutions.

Congratulations on finishing this free course. You now have a solid foundation to look at any “smart” project and ask the right human questions.
