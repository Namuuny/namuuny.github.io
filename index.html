About the Studio

Bringing harmony to urban living.

Smart City Design is a practice focused on future-oriented urban systems, storytelling, and experiences. We work at the intersection of urban planning, design, technology, and human empathy.

Our current flagship project, Urbanista X, combines deep local knowledge of Ulaanbaatar with advanced AR design to create a new kind of city guide — one that feels like a friend and looks toward a healed future.

Urban Planning & Design

Strategic thinking about how cities evolve, with a focus on regenerative and human-centered outcomes.

AR & Digital Experiences

Partnering with top AR talent to turn future visions into experiences people can actually walk through.

Storytelling for Cities

We believe the best way to care for a place is to help people fall in love with both its past and its possible futures.
