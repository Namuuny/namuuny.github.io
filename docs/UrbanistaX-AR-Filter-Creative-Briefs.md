# Urbanista X — AR Filter Creative Briefs
**For the AR Designer**  
**Brand**: Urbanista X · Smart City Design  
**Tone reference**: Futuristic techy girl · playful + sophisticated · happy, optimistic, caring, empathetic, cheerful, sensitive, youthful & colorful · from a healed future · academic but warm  

**Overall technical notes**:
- Soft, luminous, never harsh or aggressive neon
- High performance on mid-range phones (Instagram / TikTok Spark AR or equivalent)
- Character should feel friendly and slightly translucent / holographic when appearing
- Color system: Electric Teal `#2DD4BF`, Soft Violet `#A78BFA`, Warm Coral `#FB7185`, Deep Indigo backgrounds
- Always include subtle “friend energy” — soft particles, gentle glow, never cold

---

## Filter 1 — “Urbanista X Companion”
**Primary use**: Awareness, waitlist, character introduction  
**Platforms**: Instagram, TikTok  

**User experience**:
1. User opens filter.
2. Soft particle bloom.
3. Urbanista X appears beside or slightly behind the user (world-tracked or face-attached soft presence).
4. She turns, smiles gently, and gives a small wave or nod.
5. Floating text or voice line option: “Hi. I’ve been waiting to walk with you.” or “The future starts today.”
6. Optional share sticker: “Walking with Urbanista X”

**Visual style**:
- Character design: youthful, elegant, slightly otherworldly but approachable. Soft neon edge lighting (teal/violet). Academic yet colorful clothing details (subtle future-tech patterns).
- No heavy sci-fi armor. Think “friend who happens to be from 2140.”
- Particles: soft glowing orbs that drift slowly.

**Success metric**: High try-on rate + share rate. Users should feel “she’s with me.”

---

## Filter 2 — “Future Overlay”
**Primary use**: Hunt teasers, location-based content, education  

**User experience**:
1. User points camera at a building, plaza, or open space.
2. Surface detection triggers a soft scan animation (teal/violet lines).
3. A translucent “future version” of the architecture fades in over the real one.
4. Short floating caption or voice: “This is one possible tomorrow.”
5. User can take a photo that shows both layers.

**Visual style**:
- Future architecture: hopeful, climate-responsive, human-scaled, green, luminous but grounded (not flying cars fantasy).
- Transition: elegant dissolve, not hard cut.
- Keep real-world proportions so it feels achievable.

**Variants**:
- Generic “any city” version for broad reach
- Specific UB landmark versions (Sükhbaatar, Gandan, Zaisan, etc.) for campaign

---

## Filter 3 — “Empathy Aura”
**Primary use**: Emotional connection, solo traveler content, Stories  

**User experience**:
1. Soft color wash (warm coral to teal gradient) appears around the user.
2. Gentle particles orbit slowly.
3. Floating text options the user can cycle:  
   - “I’m here with you.”  
   - “It’s okay to feel small in a big city.”  
   - “You don’t have to rush.”  
   - “I’m already proud of you for exploring.”
4. Optional subtle heart or friend-orb that follows the user.

**Visual style**:
- Extremely soft and non-intrusive.
- Feels like a warm digital hug rather than a spectacle.
- Works well in low light and bright daylight.

---

## Filter 4 — “Hunt Scanner / Future Artifact”
**Primary use**: Gamification, scavenger hunt promotion, UGC  

**User experience**:
1. Camera activates a subtle scanning reticle.
2. When pointed at a designated zone (or randomly for fun), a glowing “Future Artifact” materializes and floats.
3. User can “collect” it with a tap or hold.
4. Artifact goes into a simple collection preview (seed, circuit, lens, key, star — matching the sample hunt).
5. Share card: “I found a piece of the future in Ulaanbaatar.”

**Visual style**:
- Artifacts are small, beautiful, slightly holographic objects with soft internal light.
- Collection animation is satisfying but calm (not aggressive game-reward flash).

---

## Filter 5 — “Time Echo”
**Primary use**: Educational / deeper storytelling content  

**User experience**:
1. User frames a landmark.
2. Screen gently splits or layers into three soft states:  
   - Past (desaturated or archival-tinted)  
   - Present (clear)  
   - Future (luminous teal-violet overlay)
3. User can swipe or tap to emphasize one layer.
4. Caption: “Every place holds all three times at once.”

**Visual style**:
- Elegant, almost editorial.
- Past layer should feel respectful, not spooky.
- Future layer consistent with Filter 2 language.

---

## General Delivery Notes for Designer
- Provide .arexport / project files + preview videos.
- Include both front-camera (selfie) and back-camera (world) optimized versions where relevant.
- Accessibility: high contrast text options, reduced-motion alternatives.
- Localization ready: text elements easily swappable for Mongolian / English.
- Brand safety: no dystopian imagery, no violence, no political provocation, no cultural appropriation of sacred symbols.
- Character consistency: if a full character model is created, lock proportions, face, and color language across all filters.

**Reference feeling**:  
“A brilliant, kind friend from a better future just appeared beside me and made the city feel less lonely and more full of possibility.”

Questions or iteration requests welcome. Let’s make her feel real.
