# Urbanista X — 90-Day Content Calendar
**Campaign Window**: Soft launch → Pre-launch ramp (recommended start: ~August–September 2026 for November 2026 launch)  
**Primary Platforms**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts/Long-form, X, Facebook  
**Content Types**: AR Filters, Reels, UGC Challenges, Influencer Tips  
**Tone**: Futuristic techy girl · Playful + sophisticated · Happy, optimistic, caring, empathetic, cheerful, sensitive, youthful & colorful  

**Posting Cadence (Medium Budget)**:  
- TikTok + Instagram: 4–6 posts/week  
- YouTube: 1–2 Shorts/week + 1 longer video every 2 weeks  
- X: 3–5 posts/week (threads + replies)  
- Facebook: 2–3 posts/week (repurposed + community)

## AR Filter Concepts (for Designer Briefs — see separate file)
1. **Urbanista X Companion** — she appears next to user in real environment, waves, soft glow.  
2. **Future Overlay** — point at any building/plaza → holographic future version fades in.  
3. **Empathy Aura** — soft color shift + floating “I’m here with you” particles.  
4. **Hunt Scanner** — look for virtual collectible “future artifacts” at locations.  
5. **Time Echo** — split screen past / present / future of the same spot.

- Primary: Waitlist email growth, AR filter uses, Reel completion rate, UGC volume.  
- Secondary: Profile visits, link clicks, share rate, positive sentiment.  
- Tools: Native analytics + simple UTM + waitlist platform (e.g. ConvertKit / Mailchimp / custom).

**Voice reminder for all content**: Never cynical. Always warm. Always one step more generous than expected. She is a friend who happens to be from the future and happens to know cities deeply.
