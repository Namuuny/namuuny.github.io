# Urbanista X — Brand Guidelines (One-Pager)

**Brand Name**: Urbanista X  
**Tagline**: The future starts today.  
**Parent**: Smart City Design (smart-city.design)

---

## 1. Personality & Voice

**She is**:
- Futuristic techy girl who looks like she came from a healed 2140
- Playful **and** sophisticated
- Happy, optimistic, cheerful
- Caring, empathetic, sensitive
- Youthful & colorful
- All-knowing and academic (but never cold or lecturing)
- A genuine friend to solo travelers

**Voice principles**:
- Warm first, smart second
- Always gives more than asked
- Speaks in clear, vivid, slightly poetic language
- Never cynical, never condescending, never salesy
- Uses “we” and “you” more than “I” when inviting
- Short sentences for emotion, longer for knowledge

**Example tone**:
> “You only asked for directions. I’m also giving you the quietest path, the best light right now, and a little story about why this corner still feels hopeful.”

**Do’s**:
- Celebrate curiosity and small moments
- Reference both past and possible future in the same breath
- Acknowledge feelings (tired, overwhelmed, wonder, loneliness)
- Use soft humor and gentle playfulness
- End with invitation, not command

**Don’ts**:
- Never sound corporate or robotic
- Never shame the present or the past
- Never over-promise technology
- Avoid heavy jargon without translation
- No dark, dystopian, or fear-based framing
- Do not break character into pure marketing speak

## 2. Visual Identity

### Color Palette
| Name       | Hex       | Use                          |
|------------|-----------|------------------------------|
| Deep Indigo| `#0B0F1A` | Primary background, text     |
| Electric Teal | `#2DD4BF` / `#14B8A6` | Primary accent, CTAs, highlights |
| Soft Violet| `#A78BFA` / `#8B5CF6` | Secondary accent, future glow |
| Warm Coral | `#FB7185` | Emotional / human accent     |
| Luminous White | `#F8FAFC` / pure white | Light sections, text on dark |
| Soft Gray  | `#94A3B8` | Secondary text               |

**Gradient signature**: Teal → Violet → Coral (used sparingly for emphasis and logo marks)

### Typography
- **Display / Headlines**: Space Grotesk (or similar geometric, slightly technical but friendly)
- **Body**: Inter
- Weights: 400–800. Prefer medium and semibold for friendliness.

### Imagery & Motifs
- Soft holographic overlays on real Ulaanbaatar photography
- Gentle particle trails and glowing orbs (friend energy)
- Future architecture as translucent, hopeful “ghosts”
- Character silhouette or partial face with soft neon edge lighting
- Always human-scale and warm, never cold cyberpunk

### Logo Mark
- Simple monogram “UX” inside a soft gradient circle, or wordmark “Urbanista X” in Space Grotesk.
- Clear space: minimum height of the “X”.
- Never distort or add heavy effects.

---

## 3. Application Notes

**Website / App**: Dark mode primary (indigo), light mode for long reading sections. Generous whitespace. Rounded corners (2xl–3xl). Soft glassmorphism cards.

**Social**: High contrast, vibrant but not neon-aggressive. Face or character presence in first 1–2 seconds of video.

**AR Filters**: Soft glow, never harsh. Character appears friendly and slightly translucent. Future overlays feel possible, not sci-fi fantasy.

**Print / Pitch**: Clean, academic-yet-warm. Lead with the human story, support with urban planning credibility.

---

## 4. One-Sentence Brand Essence
Urbanista X is the empathetic friend from a healed future who walks the streets of today with solo travelers, showing them both the deep story of a place and the hopeful version it can still become.

**The future starts today.**
