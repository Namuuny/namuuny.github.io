AR Experience Urban Storytelling Ulaanbaatar Launching Nov 2026

Urbanista X

An empathetic AI + AR companion for solo travelers. She reveals the soul of Ulaanbaatar — its history, its people, and the regenerative future that is already beginning.

Concept visualization of Urbanista X in Ulaanbaatar

The vision

Urbanista X is more than a digital guide. She is a warm, culturally grounded companion who walks with you through the streets of Ulaanbaatar. Using AR overlays, she shares layered stories — from ancient caravan routes and socialist-era planning decisions to emerging green corridors and community-led regeneration projects.

Designed first for solo travelers and curious locals, she combines real-time navigation, historical context, and playful scavenger hunts that invite people to imagine and care for the city’s possible futures.

The project sits at the intersection of human-centered urban design (inspired by Gehl principles), local knowledge, and accessible AR technology. It aims to deepen emotional connection to place — because people protect what they love.

What she offers

Living History

Layered stories of urban planning decisions, nomadic heritage, and everyday life that shaped the city you walk through.

Real-time Guidance

Gentle navigation that prioritizes human-scale streets, public spaces, and unexpected green pockets.

Gamified Hunts

Playful scavenger missions that unlock future visions of neighborhoods and invite personal reflection.

Empathetic Presence

An AI companion that feels like a knowledgeable, kind local friend — never a cold tour script.

Design Approach

Human-scale technology for a regenerative city

Urbanista X is built on the belief that the best digital tools for cities are the ones that help people fall in love with the physical places around them.

  • → Deep local research into Ulaanbaatar’s urban morphology, public life, and community stories
  • → Gehl-inspired focus on life between buildings and human experience over abstract “smart” metrics
  • → Partnership with AR specialists to keep the technology invisible and the storytelling primary
  • → Iterative testing with solo travelers and residents to ensure cultural resonance and emotional safety
