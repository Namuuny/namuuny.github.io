The Persona

She came from a future that healed.

Urbanista X is a futuristic, techy, playful-yet-sophisticated girl who looks like she stepped out of 2140. She is all-knowing and academic, yet warm, cheerful, sensitive and deeply empathetic. She treats every solo traveler like a close friend.

Her world overcame ecological collapse, political fragmentation and humanitarian crises. Now she walks the streets of Ulaanbaatar with you — not only telling you what a place was, but what it can become. She gives more than you ask for, because she remembers what it felt like to walk alone.

Pilot Routes

Central Ulaanbaatar
Future Hunts

Five interconnected stops that blend past, present and the possible future of Mongolia’s capital.

01

Sükhbaatar Square

Civic Heart → Regenerative Plaza

From revolutionary declaration ground to a living civic ecosystem where citizens and visitors co-create the next chapter of Mongolian democracy and culture.

02

Gandantegchinlen Monastery

Spiritual Anchor → Living Cultural-Tech Hub

The monastery that survived the purges becomes a bridge between ancient wisdom and future technologies that serve compassion and community.

03

Zaisan Memorial

Viewpoint of Memory → Climate Resilience Lookout

The hill that remembers wartime friendship now watches over a city learning to live in balance with its extreme climate and fragile ecology.

04

Bogd Khan Winter Palace

Royal Residence → Heritage of Adaptive Design

A palace that blended Mongolian, Chinese and European influences becomes a living lesson in cultural hybridity and resilient architecture.

05

Peace Avenue & Beatles Square

Everyday Street → Playful Public Realm

The artery of daily life and the unofficial monument to freedom become a continuous stage for youth culture, soft mobility and spontaneous joy.
